Privacy Policy for PickDuo

Last updated: September 29, 2026

PickDuo is a casual mobile game developed and published by VT Hyper Casual Games Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the PickDuo mobile game.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

PickDuo stores certain game information locally on your device, such as your best score. This information is used to provide game functionality and preserve your progress.

Locally stored game data is not directly transmitted to the developer solely through this local storage functionality.

2. Game Services and Leaderboards

PickDuo may use platform gaming services, including Apple Game Center and/or Google Play Games Services, to provide features such as player authentication and leaderboards.

When you use these services, certain information may be processed by Apple or Google according to their respective privacy policies and account settings. This may include information associated with your gaming profile, player identifier, scores, achievements, or other information required to provide gaming services.

PickDuo may submit gameplay scores to supported leaderboard services. The developer does not use this information for independent advertising or user profiling.

3. Advertising

The current version of PickDuo does not integrate or display third-party advertising.

If advertising services are introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate to describe the relevant data practices.

4. In-App Purchases

The current version of PickDuo does not provide active in-app purchase functionality. If in-app purchases are introduced in the future, transactions will be processed through the applicable platform provider, such as Google Play or Apple's App Store, and this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

5. Analytics and Tracking

The current version of PickDuo does not integrate third-party analytics or advertising tracking SDKs for the purpose of tracking users across apps or websites.

6. Personal Information

PickDuo does not require users to create a separate PickDuo account and does not directly request information such as a user's name, home address, phone number, or payment card details.

Information processed through Apple Game Center or Google Play Games Services is subject to the privacy practices of those respective service providers.

7. Children's Privacy

PickDuo does not knowingly request personal information directly from children through a PickDuo account or registration system. Platform services used by the game may apply their own age requirements, parental controls, and privacy practices.

8. Third-Party Services

PickDuo may interact with services provided by third parties, including:

  • Apple Game Center
  • Google Play Games Services, where supported
  • Apple App Store
  • Google Play

These third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to minimize the collection and processing of user information. Game information stored locally on your device is subject to the security protections of your device and operating system.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PickDuo adds, removes, or changes features or third-party services. Any changes will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
